Case Study

Real Pinterest Ads. Real sales.

One live conversion campaign I ran on Pinterest for an e-commerce brand, straight from Pinterest Ads Manager.

$168Ad spend
$11.27KTotal order value
67×Return on ad spend
What does 67× mean?For every $1 spent on ads, this campaign brought back about $67 in orders ($168 in ad spend → $11.27K in order value). Many e-commerce brands aim for a ROAS of 3–4×, so 67× is far beyond that target. ROAS compares order value to ad spend; it is not profit.

My contribution

  • Set up the Pinterest business account, product catalog and domain verification
  • Set up conversion tracking so purchases are measured
  • Designed the ad creatives
  • Built an always-on conversions campaign aimed at purchases, not just clicks
